Research Overview

This study projects end-of-life (EOL) PV waste generation in Ibaraki Prefecture and its allocation across 44 municipalities, comparing installation and module-retirement scenarios through 2050.

Key Findings

Delayed scenario effects. Differences between BAU and CN become more pronounced toward 2050; near-term waste is largely driven by historical installations.

Municipal priorities. Tsukuba, Mito, Kasama and Kamisu consistently rank among the top four municipalities in projected annual EOL PV waste in 2050.

Planning implications. Municipal estimates can help guide PV waste collection, temporary storage and recycling infrastructure planning.

Municipal projections reflect fixed installation shares within PV system-size categories.
